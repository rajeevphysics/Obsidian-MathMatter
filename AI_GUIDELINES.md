## Using AI Ethically in Contributions

AI tools (like ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, etc.) can be helpful for drafting explanations, code, or LaTeX.  
However, contributors must follow these guidelines:

### 1. AI as a Draft, Not a Final Product
- AI output should **always be reviewed and edited** before adding it to the vault.  
- Verify correctness with textbooks, lecture notes, or reliable academic sources.  

### 2. Accuracy First
- Concepts must prioritize **accuracy over speed**.  
- Equations, derivations, and definitions generated by AI must be double-checked.  

### 3. No Blind Copy-Pasting
- Copy-pasting large chunks of unedited AI text is not acceptable.  
- Always rewrite in your own words or adapt into the project’s style.  

### 4. Respect Sources
- If AI output uses text that closely mirrors a textbook, article, or blog, provide a reference or citation.  
- Don’t use AI to bypass academic integrity standards.  

---

🔎 **In short:** AI is a tool to speed up thinking, not a replacement for thinking.  
Every contribution must reflect human judgment, clarity, and verification.
